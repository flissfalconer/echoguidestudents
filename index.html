About EchoGuide Free

EchoGuide listens to you read aloud and highlights the next word in your script. It is designed so you can rehearse with your own words on stage; no loose pages, no AI chat.

Privacy: EchoGuide does not record or store your audio. Your scripts are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Browser: Speech recognition works best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop or Android device. Safari has limited support.
